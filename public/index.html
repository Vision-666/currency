How does the currency converter work?

Enter an amount, choose the currency you have, and choose the currency you need. k4currency uses live rates from a public foreign-exchange data provider and updates the result when you change the source currency.

What is a mid-market exchange rate?

The mid-market rate is the midpoint between the buy and sell prices in global currency markets. Banks and payment providers may add their own margin or fees.

Which currencies can I convert?

The converter includes USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, CNY, INR, and CHF. Select a source and target currency to see the current conversion rate.
